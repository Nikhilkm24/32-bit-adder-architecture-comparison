## 2. PPA Trade-Off Analysis

### Performance (Speed / Data Arrival Time):
* **Brent–Kung (BKA)** demonstrated the lowest critical path arrival time (**1.675 ns**), achieving:
  * **~23.38% faster** data arrival than Ripple-Carry.
  * **~17.93% faster** data arrival than Kogge-Stone.
* **Kogge–Stone (KSA)** achieved **2.041 ns** data arrival (~6.63% faster than RCA).
* **Ripple-Carry (RCA)** required **2.186 ns** due to serial carry traversal.

### Silicon Area & Cell Count:
* **Ripple-Carry (RCA)** and **Han-Carlson (HCA)** achieved the smallest cell footprint (**161 cells, 195.776 library units**).
* **Brent–Kung (BKA)** required **190 cells (219.716 units)**, representing a **+12.23% area increase** over RCA to achieve its 23.4% timing gain.
* **Kogge–Stone (KSA)** used **179 cells (214.130 units)**.

### Power Dissipation (Dynamic + Leakage):
* **RCA** achieved the lowest overall power consumption (**17.883 μW**) due to having fewer active internal switching nodes.
* **HCA** consumed **18.976 μW**, saving **~8.3% power** compared to Brent-Kung and Kogge-Stone.
* **BKA and KSA** exhibited the highest power consumption (**~20.64 μW – 20.71 μW**) due to higher internal switching activity and larger total cell gate capacitances.

### Area-Delay Product (ADP):
* **Brent–Kung** achieved the best overall figure of merit with an **ADP of 368.024** (lowest among all 4 designs), indicating that its speed advantage outweighed its modest area penalty.

## 3. Key ASIC & Architectural Insights

1. **Gate-Level Reality vs. Theoretical Depth:** In theoretical arithmetic textbooks, Kogge–Stone has fewer logic levels (5 prefix stages) than Brent–Kung (9 prefix stages). However, at the gate level, cell sizing, fan-out buffering, and synthesis restructuring can cause sparse architectures like Brent–Kung to outperform dense ones.
2. **Power & Fan-out Advantage of Sparsity:** Architectures with lower wiring and node counts (RCA and Han-Carlson) reduce parasitic capacitive loading and static leakage power.
3. **Formal Equivalence:** All four adders have been mathematically proven across the entire $2^{65}$ state space using Yosys SAT verification, ensuring zero functional bugs.
